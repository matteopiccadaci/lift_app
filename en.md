# Lift Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 31, 2026*

This policy covers the **Lift** app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

## In short

- Lift needs no account and has no servers of its own: your data stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud storage.
- No data reaches the developer: Lift contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
- Apple Health data is read and written only with your permission and never goes to iCloud.

## 1. Data controller

Matteo Piccadaci, email [matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com](mailto:matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com).

Processing complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

## 2. Data stored on your devices

- **Profile:** first and last name; date of birth, height and weight, entered by you or read from Apple Health with your permission.
- **Routines and workouts:** exercises, sets, reps, loads, durations and distances, techniques and grips, notes, favorites, dates and times of your sets.
- **Where it is kept:** in the app's storage on iPhone and Apple Watch. iPhone and Apple Watch exchange data directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, without going through any server.
- **Backups and exports:** the files you create (export, clipboard, AirDrop, or a folder you choose, such as one in iCloud Drive) stay under your control. If you use iPhone backup (to iCloud or a computer), Lift's data is included like that of any other app.

## 3. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone and have not turned off iCloud for Lift, Lift saves a copy of your routines, exercises, workouts, notes, favorites and profile name in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account (CloudKit). This lets you find them on a new iPhone or after reinstalling the app, and keeps them in step across your devices.

- Apple keeps this copy in your iCloud account, under the iCloud terms and privacy policy. The developer of Lift cannot read it: it does not go through any Lift server and is not visible to third parties.
- Date of birth, height and weight stay on the device only: they do not go to iCloud, like all Apple Health data.
- You can turn off iCloud for Lift in iPhone Settings, in the iCloud section of your account: your data stays on the device and stops syncing.
- Without iCloud, Lift works the same way, with your data on the device only.

## 4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Lift uses Apple Health only if you allow it.

- **Data read:** date of birth, height and weight, to fill in your profile, only after you tap "Connect Apple Health" in the Profile; workouts recorded by Lift, to show them in Progress; on Apple Watch, during a workout, heart rate and active and resting energy, for live statistics.
- **Data written:** completed workouts (activity type, start, end and duration) with active and resting energy.

Health data never leaves your devices: Lift does not send it to servers, to third parties or to iCloud. It is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and never sold or shared with third parties: it is used only to track your workouts in Lift.

## 5. Data that leaves your device

Only the iCloud copy described in section 3, in your own account. Lift sends no data to servers of its own or to third parties. If a future feature ever needs to, this policy will be updated before that feature becomes available.

## 6. No ads, no tracking

Lift contains no advertising, uses no third-party analytics or profiling libraries, and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- You can delete routines, exercises and sets in the app at any time: with iCloud on, the deletion also applies to the iCloud copy and to your other devices.
- Uninstalling the app removes the data from the device; the iCloud copy remains until you delete it from iCloud storage management in iPhone Settings (Lift entry).
- From the Diary you can delete a workout from both Lift and Apple Health; in the Health app you can manage or delete everything Lift wrote there.
- Lift keeps no data on its own servers and has no access to your iCloud copy. For any request about your data, write to [matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com](mailto:matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com): we reply within 30 days, free of charge.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15-22) you can request access to your data, rectification, erasure and portability: Lift's export produces a file in an open format (JSON), and you manage the iCloud copy from your Apple Account. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali ([www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is available in the app (Profile, Privacy Policy) and on its web page, with the date of the update.
