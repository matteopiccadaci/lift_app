# Lift Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2, 2026*

This policy covers the **Lift** app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

## In short

- Lift needs no account and has no servers: your data stays on your devices.
- No data reaches the developer: the app sends nothing over the internet and contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
- Apple Health data is read and written only with your permission and never leaves your devices.

## 1. Data controller

Matteo Piccadaci, email [matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com](mailto:matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com).

Processing complies with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

## 2. Data stored on your devices

- **Profile:** first and last name; date of birth, height and weight, entered by you or read from Apple Health with your permission.
- **Routines and workouts:** exercises, sets, reps, loads, durations and distances, techniques and grips, notes, favorites, dates and times of your sets.
- **Where it is kept:** in the app's storage on iPhone and Apple Watch. iPhone and Apple Watch exchange data directly through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, without going through any server.
- **Backups and exports:** the files you create (export, clipboard, AirDrop, or a folder you choose, such as one in iCloud Drive) stay under your control. If you use iPhone backup (to iCloud or a computer), Lift's data is included like that of any other app.

## 3. Apple Health (HealthKit)

Lift uses Apple Health only if you allow it.

- **Data read:** date of birth, height and weight, to fill in your profile, only after you tap "Connect Apple Health" in the Profile; workouts recorded by Lift, to show them in Progress; on Apple Watch, during a workout, heart rate and active and resting energy, for live statistics.
- **Data written:** completed workouts (activity type, start, end and duration) with active and resting energy.

Health data never leaves your devices: Lift does not send it to servers, to third parties or to iCloud. It is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and never sold or shared with third parties: it is used only to track your workouts in Lift.

## 4. Data that leaves your device

None. The iPhone and Apple Watch app does not connect to servers and sends no data over the internet. If a future feature ever needs to, this policy will be updated before that feature becomes available.

## 5. No ads, no tracking

Lift contains no advertising, uses no third-party analytics or profiling libraries, and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- You can delete routines, exercises and sets in the app at any time; uninstalling it removes all the data it keeps on the device.
- From the Diary you can delete a workout from both Lift and Apple Health; in the Health app you can manage or delete everything Lift wrote there.
- Lift keeps no data on its own servers. For any request about your data, write to [matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com](mailto:matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com): we reply within 30 days, free of charge.

## 7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15-22) you can request access to your data, rectification, erasure and portability: Lift's export produces a file in an open format (JSON). You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali ([www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is available in the app (Profile, Privacy Policy) and on its web page, with the date of the update.

## Contact and support

For questions, problems or suggestions about Lift, write to [matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com](mailto:matteopiccadaci02@gmail.com).
